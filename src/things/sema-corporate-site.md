I redesigned the Sema Software corporate website as a gateway into the upcoming React web app, with an emphasis on solution-selling and converting visitors into booked demos.

![Fairly simple SaaSy design](/public/things/sema-corporate-site/semasoftware.com_screens01.png)

Fairly simple SaaSy design

I designed it in Figma and built it in [Webflow](https://webflow.com/), including a CMS-driven solutions section with auto-generated landing pages for sales outreach. The illustrations and iconography established a new design language that carried over into printed documents and presentations.
