### Picking a direction

We narrowed in on this minimal design composed of a scented ceramic material, sheet metal clip and soft cork gripping surface. The materials felt substantial, not cheap like other products on the shelf, and it was fairly straightforward to manufacture and disassemble. Here are just a few of the material finishes explored.
